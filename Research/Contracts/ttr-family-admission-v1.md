# TTR training family admission v1

**Purpose.** Maximum-mini-NUIAK qualifies a native Fixture recipe family or SDK app route once, then admits eligible frames through automated checks. This implements H2 of [TRAIN666](../Plans/Train666.md) under the [model improvement loop](../ModelImprovementLoop/SKILL.md). Human annotation time goes to the real-app benchmark.

## Qualify a family

1. Identify the family by producer, construction and recipe or app route, projection version, and the geometry and state behavior actually exercised. Record tested revision and use restrictions. Changes to colors, strings or assets can stay within the same family when ownership and geometry semantics remain the same. Changes to ownership, projection, transforms, clipping or occlusion semantics require qualification of the affected scope.
2. Bind a clean Q-packet to its manifest digest and referenced screenshot, sidecar, answer key and independent geometry. Record the exact hash basis for multi-file packets. Verify frozen keys for visible objects, roles, owners, omissions and observed focus. A missing observation is unknown. Qualify only the tested states and target constructions. Use the frozen [ten-role projection](tvos-framework-projection-v1.md).
3. Exercise every declared corrupted twin against its named failure check. A generic exception, a clean frame incorrectly rejected, a missing applicable twin, or a wrong reason leaves the affected scope pending. Retain a single reproducible evidence receipt per family. The established checks are in [qpacket_import604.py](../../scripts/qpacket_import604.py).
4. Write the projected labels and reopen them through the normal training loader. Match identities/classes one-to-one and invert resize and letterboxing to source pixels. Require the maximum decoded edge difference from the written reference to be at most 1 source pixel. Record independent visible-body agreement separately using the established geometry contract. A serialization pass alone cannot establish where a visible control ends. See [milestone_readback604.py](../../scripts/milestone_readback604.py).
5. Register the outcome, supporting packet digests and measured scope in [families.json](../../reports/work/TTR666/families.json). Family qualification establishes label behavior. Each frame still inherits its original data role, permitted use and group. Benchmark, sealed, review-only, diagnostic-only and unassigned real-app inputs cannot enter training through this rule. A whole-app role change requires Joe's approval.

## Admit subsequent batches

Run the existing importer on every frame, enforcing its identity, provenance, projection, ownership and visibility checks. Retain omissions and unknowns. For the frozen accepted batch of each qualified family, take a reproducible 1% audit without replacement. One implementation is to record an audit seed before inspection, sort distinct frame IDs by SHA-256 of the seed, family ID and frame ID (UTF-8, newline-delimited), and take the first ceiling(N/100) of N eligible frames. Record the seed, membership digest, sampled IDs, denominator and result. Repeat the comparison with independent geometry and the normal loader on that sample. Audit sampling provides defect detection; it does not establish perfect labels or independent layouts.

Keep a batch receipt and exceptions. Routine admitted frames need no individual reconciliation report or human sign-off. Isolate a failed scope and record the rejected reason before producing a corrected successor; other qualified scopes can proceed. Retain layout groups across all variations for splitting. Publish training or transfer gains only from the frozen, human-reviewed real-app benchmark.
