  supply additive schema, positive/invalid examples and platform capability matrix.
  Export actual post-layout geometry, full visible inventory, native focus, selected
  state, ancestry and before/after frame correlation. Separate wrapper/artwork/label
  and presentation-effect geometry; do not require artwork bounds for buttons.
