## Common contract

- Use development data for diagnosis. These inspected cases cannot become untouched final evaluation.
- Keep routes, related artwork, derivatives, and reversed pairs in connected groups.
- Preserve full frames, original crops, recipe hashes, asset hashes, and model hashes.
- Record installed binaries separately from matching source evidence.
- Record body bounds separately from glow, shadow, and artwork bounds.
- Treat capture intervals as correlation, not authenticated framebuffer identity.
- Keep native accessibility settings separate from authored appearance presets.
- Record unsupported themes and layouts. Do not substitute another theme.
- Use 5 to 10 compatible jobs per cycle, with 800 s and 100 MB per diagnostic job.
- Acquire inputs separately from the generic diagnostic runner. Keep target ownership and restoration checks explicit.
- Prepare the Simulator once per bounded campaign. Do not restart it between experiments.
- Cache predictions by complete input, preprocessing, model, and settings identities.

## First native delivery

Request 12 development pairs from one complete LUMA path within 10 min and 100 MB.
Include movement, fixed-focus content changes, stable repeats, and Back restoration when supported.
Require one valid versioned capture example before accepting a campaign.
Stop on missing observations, changed runtime identity, or uncertain cleanup.
Preserve partial evidence. Do not retry unchanged failures automatically.
