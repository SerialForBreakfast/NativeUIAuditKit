## Isolated graph steps

1. Freeze the retained native cases and score the fixed outline rule.
2. Capture 8 parts, grouped by role. Each part contains at most 50 scenes.
3. Check each part before the next capture starts. Reject missing labels, changed images, and failed restoration.
4. Require visible profile differences near the observed focus before admitting each matched state.
5. Make production crops in 6 parts. Preserve original images and crop geometry.
6. Combine verified membership. Reject duplicate states and role conflicts.
7. Score the fixed rule and retained crop model on matched development and regression cases.
8. Train each candidate separately with frozen initialization and preprocessing.
9. Score each candidate on the same cases. Save raw predictions, labels, hashes, and counts.
10. Report gains, losses, wrong selections, abstentions, and support. Keep model acceptance separate from job completion.

Each step writes a durable result. Capture and training publish actual progress events for the dashboard.

## Comparisons and remaining gates

The outline rule uses full-frame pixels. It does not use observed focus to select a candidate.
The crop comparison uses observed control boxes. This isolates classification; it does not establish deployable proposal recall.
The full-screen detector predicts its own box. Compare decisions at body IoU of at least 0.5 and retain raw geometry.
Keep HCF and Standard results separate. Do not interpret Standard failures as HCF failures.
No-focus false selections remain unavailable until qualified no-focus examples exist.
The final report requires all 4 methods. Missing reports cannot produce acceptance.

After development comparison, select a candidate and freeze its settings before opening the final audit.
Then test deployable proposals, the full retained production suite, and Core ML conversion with the existing export tools.
Those gates remain explicit follow-up tasks. This graph does not claim a production-ready Core ML model.
If training times out while loss improves, qualify a longer continuation instead of repeating the same short run.
If the methods fail similarly, inspect visible effects and control coverage before expanding volume.
