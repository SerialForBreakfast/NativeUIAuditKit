## Why

Runtime chooses a winner above0.85; the current frame gate counts all independent
positive crops. Both are meaningful but different. Diagnostic ranking does not
authorize forced selection, threshold tuning or promotion. Reused development
frames cannot establish independent confidence, especially with only13complete
frames (eight Settings-related). Calibration needs separate grouped data; keep
related recipes/journeys together. Pretraining must not inherit the legacy shortcut
of skipping pretrained weights due to a dependency problem.
