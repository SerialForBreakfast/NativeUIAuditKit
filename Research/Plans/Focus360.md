### Research checks and limits

[WiSE-FT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.01903) studies interpolation of zero-shot and fine-tuned weights. Its result does not establish NUIAK compatibility or a guaranteed gain.
[TIES](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.01708) addresses conflicting parameter updates. Treat its use here as a separate hypothesis after lineage checks.
[LogitNorm](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.09310) normalizes a vector of class logits. Direct scalar normalization is not a justified drop-in replacement.
Our algebraic check uses `z / (0.05 * abs(z) + 1e-7)`. Its derivative away from zero is `1e-7 / (0.05 * abs(z) + 1e-7)^2`.
Positive scalar magnitudes approach 20. This removes useful magnitude differences and sharply reduces gradients away from zero.
[TracIn](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.08484) traces training influence. One last-layer dot product is a local conflict diagnostic, not the complete method.
[Group-DRO](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.08731) also depends on regularization for generalization. The supplied learning rates and loss weights are hypotheses, not validated defaults.
The claimed wave duration, exact native growth law, automatic safe merge, and leakage-free family score remain unverified.

## Research support and limits

[PCGrad](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.06782) supports investigating conflicting gradients in multi-task training.
Applying it to our condition groups is a new experiment, not a proven correction.

[WiSE-FT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.01903) studies interpolation between related model weights.
Its results do not establish compatibility or improvement for our small focus models.
[TIES](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.01708) studies interference during model merging. It does not remove the need for parameter alignment.

[Group DRO](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.08731) motivates measuring poorly performing groups and controlling their training influence.
Our groups need sufficient support. More weight on a mislabeled group can increase harm.

[LogitNorm](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/wei22d.html) studies vector logit normalization.
Our scalar-output case needs separate mathematics and tests before adoption.

[Conformal prediction guidance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.07511) explains coverage assumptions and limits.
Use these methods only with their stated assumptions. Do not translate coverage into an unsupported safety guarantee.
