## Outcome

Create 5 matched comparisons using the installed research-host-TTR Fixture.
Each comparison has 1 producer and 4 consumers. These are 25 jobs, not 25 independent scientific experiments.
The first producer qualifies the capture sequence before the remaining producers enter the queue.

| Factor | Values | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Control size | 80 px and 160 px requested bodies | Does smaller control size explain missed movement? |

Requested dimensions are not measured dimensions. Retain native geometry and clipping for every capture.
The background comparison changes the control background, not the whole screen.
Custom effects remain authored effects. Do not describe them as OS HCF.

If capture fails, retain evidence and restore the original scene.
If restoration fails, keep the recovery marker and block further captures.
Do not restart the Simulator or change HCF settings.

The revised sequence waits for fresh geometry after recipe reload.
It then requests focus and applies the full endpoint checks.
It skips a focus request only when the existing observation already passes those checks.
Every load and focus action has a retained receipt.

## Decisions
