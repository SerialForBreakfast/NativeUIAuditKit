## Why these tests

Known-box crop scoring performs better, so geometry needs investigation before more training.
No new threshold, fitted inset, label, or model enters production from this batch.

## Evidence rules

The authored rings test software coordinates. They do not supply native training labels.
Retained matching uses labels only for diagnosis. It does not choose runtime boxes or orientation.
The insets report describes matched cases only. Missing proposals remain explicit.
Related cases retain their groups. Inspected cases do not become final-audit data.
Coverage reports empty regions as zero support, not measured zero accuracy.
The observer smoke test belongs to Maximum-mini-NUIAK. It cannot establish TTR integration.
Original images, crops, weights, thresholds, and previous results remain unchanged.
