## APPEAR-A — Source capability audit and bounded ground-truth pilot

**Inputs:** immutable FOCUS-VISUAL-01/02 reviews; FOCUS-VISUAL-03 scores; existing
native observation/capture runner and direct Fixture HTTP/screenshot runner;

**Authority:** offline planning/source inspection may proceed when assigned. Actual
simulator target/endpoint/control scope must be explicitly approved before capture.
No Office, Settings mutations, app installation, service restart or TTR-repo edits.

1. Audit existing renderer/control capabilities read-only against this matrix:
   colorful artwork tiles, bright unfocused tiles, gray/blank placeholders, dock vs
   grid context, focus enlargement/shadow, light/dark Photos-like primary/secondary
   buttons, neighboring focused controls and clipping. Mark supported/tested,
   source-present-but-unverified, or missing; metadata-only axes do not count.
2. Prefer the independent Fixture lane for controllable appearance variants where
   actual native focus callbacks and measured rendered bounds are available.
   Reuse supported controls; do not claim that generic white grid buttons cover
   Home artwork. A genuine missing Fixture rendering capability becomes a bounded
   producer request with one expected pair, not an assumed TTR capture dependency.
3. For actual Home/Photos, use the existing native lane only if observed focused
   identity and image/geometry correlation can be established. Requested direction,
   cursor intent, OCR or model output must not label training data. If that binding
   remains unavailable, keep captures visual-review diagnostics and explicitly
   report the native training gap; do not lower admission rules.
4. Before any approved capture, freeze a small catalog capped at24 recipe/screen
   groups and two-minute per-group operations. Include focused/unfocused states
   with bright/artwork negatives, not just the target. Reconcile supported target
   counts before launch. One failed group stops that attempt; preserve partials.
5. Capture via the existing exact-target runner, recording source/build/runtime,
   observed focus, settled geometry, intervals, bytes and postflight health. Stop
   on ownership loss, stale identity, ambiguous geometry or cleanup uncertainty.
6. Validate every paired frame, production16%/256 crop and label. Inspect new
   appearance representatives and anomalies, not already reviewed screenshots.
   Produce expected/captured/accepted/rejected counts and capability gaps.

**Tests/acceptance:** no simulator mutation in planning; dry-run validates exact
target/output/expected membership. Existing corruption, focus-binding and collision
tests stay passing; any extension needs actual-entrypoint regressions. Genuine
pilot accepted only with complete scope accounting and observed labels. Synthetic
